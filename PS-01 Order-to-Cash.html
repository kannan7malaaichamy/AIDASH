What you are approving today

Five points

  1. Strangler route, not a rewrite. The legacy core stays the system of record for orders, stock and invoices in Wave 1.
  2. ₹2.50 cr for Wave 1 now, including skills and cloud run. Wave 2 (₹0.90 cr), Wave 3 (₹0.70 cr) and contingency (₹0.70 cr) are released only at gates.
  3. No production change during peak windows, and no live data migration or batch-job change in the first 90 days.
  4. A reversible pilot: online orders in one region, shadow mode, then a 5% canary with rollback in ≤ 15 minutes.
  5. Two gates and four measures: technical go/no-go on 5 Dec 2026, monthly reviews, and a formal go/no-go on 3 Feb 2027.
