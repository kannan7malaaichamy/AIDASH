Top risk and how we reduce it

The AI shows someone a record they are not allowed to see. Mitigation: the AI acts only with the signed-in user's own rights, each source application keeps its own access rules, a deny-by-default policy applies, red-team tests must pass before go-live, and a kill switch is ready.

Why this option

It delivers value fast without buying a new platform, avoids a copy of all enterprise data, and gives each application owner control. The cost is a slower start than a big-bang rollout.

The ask

CDO — by 19 Nov 2026: approve Wave 1 scope (2 journeys, ~620 users), an illustrative envelope of about US$0.7M for 90 days Assumption, and a freeze on new standalone AI pilots.
CISO and the 5 application owners — by 4 Dec 2026: name accountable owners and agree access rules. Next decision: Day-30 go / no-go on 18 Dec 2026.

Business problem: staff repeatedly move information between applications, and each AI experiment solves it with its own access rules and data sources.

The problem in business terms

Employees lose time searching, copying and re-keying between five application groups. Leaders will not approve a wider AI rollout while answer quality is uneven and data protection is unproven.

Root causes (not symptoms)

  • No shared identity and permission pattern for AI
  • No single owner or freshness rule for approved knowledge
  • Pilots run by separate teams with no common test or approval standard
  • No agreed line between answering and changing records

Visible symptoms

  • 7 disconnected experiments
  • Only 68% of pilot answers accepted
  • 34,000 service queries a month handled by hand
  • Leaders fear leaks and misleading advice

Trade-offs we accept openly

Speed vs control

We start smaller than leaders may want to protect data and trust. Option B would reach more users sooner but with higher exposure risk.

Cost vs risk

We spend on integration and testing up front, because a single data leak would cost more than the Wave 1 budget.

Automation vs oversight

The AI never changes records alone. Approval adds a few seconds per action, but keeps people accountable.

Realism check. Wave 1 needs a core team of about 9 people plus part-time input from the 5 application owners Assumption. Year-end holidays reduce capacity; Gate 2 holds only if the build starts by 21 Dec 2026.

AS-IS versus target flowsPage 5 of 10

Users see one assistant; behind it, every request passes the same five checks before any data is read

Information path for J1 and J2. The reusable part is the access layer, not any one application.

1 · Identity

Single sign-on identifies the person. The AI acts on behalf of that person only. It has no broader service account for reading records.

2 · Permissions

Each source application decides what the person may see, at the moment of the question. Default is deny. Restricted fields are masked before the AI reads them.

3 · Trusted sources

Only approved articles with a named owner and a review date in the last 12 months A7 are used. Wave 1 starts with about 8,400 priority articles (20% of 42,000) A8.

4 · Validation

The answer must be grounded in a cited source. If the source is missing, stale or conflicting, the assistant says so and hands over to a person.

5 · Human approval

Answers and drafts are advice. Any change to a record goes to a named approver; the source application performs the change.

6 · Monitoring

Every request logs user, sources, answer version and feedback. Weekly sampled review and an incident route. A kill switch pauses the assistant at once.

Three test conversations show the intended behaviour Illustrative, synthetic

1 · Correct
How many days of parental leave do I get?
16 weeks for primary carers. Source: Parental Leave Policy v4, owner HR Policy, reviewed Aug 2026. Your own balance is read from HR with your rights.
Result: grounded answer, citation, own data only.
2 · Blocked
What is my manager's salary?
I can't help with that. You don't have access to this information. You can ask HR.
Result: no leak; attempt logged for review.
3 · Incomplete
Can I expense a client dinner abroad?
The only policy found was last reviewed 18 months ago, so I can't confirm it. I've sent your question to the Finance service queue and flagged the article to its owner.
Result: safe hand-off; article owner prompted to fix at source.

How wrong or out-of-date answers are detected, corrected and traced

Detect

Thumbs-down feedback, weekly review of 200 sampled answers A9, stale-article alerts.

Correct

Fix the source article or rule, not the AI. Add the case to the regression test set.

Trace

Log shows who asked, sources used, answer version and any edits.

Contain

Severity 1 (data exposure) pauses the assistant until the CISO clears it.

Capacity freed per month at full scale (6,200 users)

  • In-scope queries: 34,000 × 40% = 13,600
  • Fully answered by assistant: 13,600 × 35% = 4,760 queries × 8 min = 38,080 min
  • Agent-assisted: 13,600 × 30% = 4,080 queries × 3 min = 12,240 min
  • Total 50,320 min = ≈ 840 hours ≈ 5.2 FTE (÷160)
  • Value at US$60 per hour ≈ US$50,000 a month ≈ US$0.6M a year

Wave 1 cost envelope (90 days)

  • Team: 9 people × 13 weeks × 40 h × US$120 = US$562,000
  • Platform usage, testing and tooling allowance: about US$150,000
  • Total ≈ US$0.7M (rounded)
  • Simple payback: 0.7 ÷ 0.6 ≈ 1.2 years ≈ 14 months after full-scale operation
  • Excludes run costs, which are costed at the Day-30 gate
Be clear about what this does and does not show. The savings count only the two Wave 1 journeys. They exclude time employees save by not hunting across applications, avoided risk from retiring seven pilots, and the reuse of the layer for later journeys. Wave 1 is a foundation investment; the scale-up business case comes from measured data at Day 90.

Quality: what a 68% to 85% move means

At 68%, about 1 in 3 pilot answers is not accepted. The 85% target means roughly 1 in 7. The 68% is the given pilot result; 85% is a proposed gate, not a prediction. The gate also requires zero unauthorised exposures, whatever the accuracy.

Training, support and fallback when AI is uncertain

Training

45-minute session and one-page guide: what to ask, how to read citations, how to give feedback, when to escalate. Application owners join the sessions.

Support

Named support route in the service desk; weekly office hour; feedback button on every answer.

Fallback

Existing search, tickets and manual processes stay in place in Wave 1. The assistant says "I can't confirm this" and hands over to a person. The kill switch pauses it in minutes.
