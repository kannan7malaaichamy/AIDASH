Five learnings

What the team learnt, and how it will change the way we work

LEARNING 01

A whole-team effort

The entire team took part in building this. We reviewed each other’s outputs, suggested validating the AI output with other models, and shared good prompting practices for sharper output and efficient token consumption.

Peer reviewCross-model validationPrompt practices
LEARNING 02

More than 15 iterations to get it right

I went through more than 15 iterations to produce the required output. Initial versions had a lot of inconsistent data and incorrect assumptions that did not match the given context. Slowly and steadily, I refined the output by validating it manually and with other LLMs.

15+ iterationsManual validationChecked with other LLMs
LEARNING 03

Better prompting, and the power of AI

I learnt the art of better prompting and saw the power of AI. This will help me improve my productivity in day-to-day work by using the available AI tools much more effectively.

Sharper promptsEveryday productivity
LEARNING 04

AI needs expert guidance and human judgement

Without the right expert guidance, AI can assume many things and produce confidently incorrect outputs. Careful review and human judgement are required to make the output factually correct and consistent.

Confidently incorrectHuman in the loop
LEARNING 05

A new domain, chosen deliberately

I learnt new terms related to Retail through this problem statement. We deliberately took a technical problem statement to understand the technical terms better and build a strong solution with our contextual knowledge. I’m happy about the learning and the experience over these 3 days.

Retail domain termsTechnical depth3 days
